# Fundraising notes

Advice from 17 November 2026, while the library still held mostly sample content and usage was not yet measured.

## Should we tag VCs or YC in posts?

No. Tagging investors on GitHub, LinkedIn or X is a public cold pitch: most scroll past, and it can mark the project as "pre-product" before there are real numbers. A short personal message works far better than a tag.

## Where Phase 1 money (₹3–5 lakh) should come from

- **The university's incubator.** Most large universities, PU included, run one; this is the first stop.
- **Government seed grants**, such as Startup India's seed fund. These usually need a registered company and an incubator first.
- **Angels and founders** from the city or alumni network who care about students.
- **Edtech founders and operators** as advisors, who can introduce investors later.

VCs come after there is traction.

## The order to do things in

1. **Post on LinkedIn without tags:** the video, the repo link and a short story ("I built this for PU students; here's the plan for 100 universities").
2. **Hit Phase 1:** real papers in the app before exams, and a usage number such as "8,000 students used it in exam week".
3. **Send personal messages** (2–3 lines each) to 10–15 specific people: incubator heads, angels, and VC partners who have backed edtech or student-focused startups. Ask for 15 minutes of advice, not money:

> I built PU Study Hub; X students used it last exam season. 65-second pitch: [link]. Could I get 15 minutes of your advice?

## Y Combinator

- Don't tag YC; nobody reviews tagged posts. Apply at **ycombinator.com/apply**. It's free and open at the idea stage.
- Apply early. A later application that shows progress since the first one counts in your favour.
- Check YC's current deal terms and batch dates on its website before applying; they change.

**What YC will like**

- A product already shipped and live, not just a deck
- A real, daily pain felt by millions of students
- A founder who is the user
- A big market (about 4.5 crore students) and a clear path from one university to many

**What YC will push on**

1. **Usage numbers.** The most important gap. Get real papers live for the next exam season and measure weekly students who open a document.
2. **Solo founder.** YC funds solo founders but asks about it; a committed co-founder strengthens the application.
3. **Revenue in India.** Be ready to explain why colleges and Plus subscribers will pay when students pay little.
4. **Copyright.** Have a clear answer: link first, host with permission, take down within 48 hours.

**For the application**

- YC asks for a **1-minute video of the founders talking**. Record it on a phone, explaining what you built and why. Don't use the produced pitch video there.
- Do include the live app link, this repo and the pitch video as demo and extra links.
