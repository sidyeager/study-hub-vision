Business model

Free core forever; money from extras and institutions

From 2029

Study Hub Plus

₹49 a month or ₹299 a year. Unlimited AI, offline exam packs, an exam planner.

From 2030

Campus partnerships

₹25,000 to ₹1 lakh a year per institution. Verified notice channel and usage reports.

From 2029

Sponsored listings

Labelled scholarships and internships, matched by course, never by tracking.

2031 model: 3% of 1 million students on Plus ≈ ₹90 lakh, plus 100 partners ≈ ₹50 lakh, gives ≈ ₹1.4 crore, about equal to planned costs. Prices are assumptions to test.

Papers, syllabus and notices stay free forever. Revenue comes from three places: an optional Plus plan, partnerships with colleges that want a verified way to reach students, and clearly labelled opportunities like scholarships. In the 2031 model those roughly cover costs. The prices are guesses we will test.
