The problem

Exam week starts with a hunt for the right papers

Scattered

Papers live on portals, notice boards, WhatsApp groups and in photocopy shops.

Unreliable

Forwarded copies conflict, years go missing, and scans are hard to read on a phone.

Last-minute

Datesheets change at short notice, and nobody gets an alert.

Generic AI

Chatbots answer without knowing your university’s syllabus.

Every student knows this week. You need the last five years of papers for tomorrow’s exam, and they are spread across five places, half of them blurry photos. Datesheets move and you hear about it from a friend. And a general chatbot doesn’t know what your syllabus actually covers.
